# Mentl through the lens of the vision — the whole tree, and three designs (2026-09-25)

**What this is.** The audit (`docs/PROCESS-AUDIT-2026-09-25.md`) measured the
process. This document does the other thing Morgan asked for: it looks at
every part of the tree through the vision the early documents state (the
medium proposes, proves, teaches, explores in parallel, and raises the person
at the keyboard), and then it takes the three questions the vision cannot
leave open and designs answers rather than reporting that nobody has them.
Every claim about the artifact was read from the artifact this session; every
design is marked as a design, with the invariant it keeps, the gate that
would see it red, and the proof it still owes. The negation design was
handed to an adversarial pass before this was committed; its findings are in
§7.

**Papert's promise, since the audit used the phrase.** Seymour Papert's
*Mindstorms* (1980) argued that a computer can be an object to think with: a
medium in which powerful ideas become things a learner builds, runs, breaks
and fixes, so that the ideas are learned the way a child in France learns
French, by living in them. Logo's turtle was the first such medium; the
promise was that learning to program in a well-made medium teaches thinking
itself, that a bug is information rather than failure, and that the learner
teaches the computer and is taught by the attempt. The promise was only
partly kept: the Logo studies found modest transfer, and the environments
that followed (Scratch, the constructionist line) kept the feedback loop but
not the powerful ideas. Mentl's claim that picking up the language teaches
programming is that promise with proof as the feedback: the gradient is the
turtle's immediate response, the Why chain is the bug as information, and a
refusal that names the claim it could not discharge is the medium teaching
the learner back. Nobody has measured an environment on that promise; §4.4
of the audit lists the measurement.

**The finding the map makes.** The medium is about ten percent medium. The
mass is a compiler being a compiler (substrate, judgment, codegen, spine:
roughly 45,000 lines) and a process being a process (8,300 lines of gates,
300,000 words of prose). The parts that are the vision's differentiators, the
proposer, the gradient, the cursor and the voice, total about 5,000 lines and
have no test of their quality. That is not a criticism of the compiler work;
a medium that proposes needs a judgment that is right, and the judgment is
right. It is the direction of marginal investment: every next hour that goes
into restructuring the judgment for its own sake is an hour not spent on the
five thousand lines where the vision lives, and the five thousand lines have
nothing to be measured on because there is no program to propose into.

**Specific findings, by part.**

- `synth_proposer`: the proof gate is real (`candidate_proven` unifies the
  candidate's cell with the hole's cell and discharges the refinement inside
  a checkpoint, then rolls back). The candidate space is shallow: vocabulary
  calls are zero-argument only, so a hole of function type in a pipeline can
  be filled by `f` but never by `f(cfg)` or `x |> f`; there is no composition
  and no bounded sub-hole recursion, so the medium cannot propose a
  two-step move. Ranking is proximity alone. The divergence classifier is
  the most original piece in the file and it reads live.
- `cursor`: the argmax is built, holes outrank annotations, proximity decays
  by scope. The "cursor moves itself" demo needs only a transport that
  re-renders on graph delta; the substrate is there.
- `mentl`: the gradient proposes annotations from a fixed lattice (Pure,
  !Alloc, !IO, !Network, own, ref, refinements) by speculative apply, verify
  and rollback. Right mechanism, small lattice, no capability the developer
  did not already know about; the proactive gradient the field lacks is here
  in seed form.
- `egraph`: six rules, directed to cheaper forms, effect-gated. The comment
  calls it equality saturation; it is a simplifier whose union-find has one
  member per class (§3).
- `effects` + the gate in `infer`: the crown is careful and its instance law
  is genuinely beyond Flix (bare versus instance, provable distinctness). Its
  open class is one shape, measured: a declared negation on a function whose
  body row is a parameter's free row variable is checked against nothing
  (`fn run(f) with !E = f()` accepts `run(() => op())`, and the program
  runs). §2 designs the fix. **And the crown's own refusal is not armed:**
  `fn f() with !E = op()` prints `E_EffectMismatch` and the executable runs
  anyway (measured, exit 7), because `diag_refuses` in `types.mn` arms
  `E_PurityViolated` and twenty-four other classes but not
  `E_EffectMismatch`. The project's arming law is that a class refuses once
  the wheel's own census of it is zero, and `mentl check src/main.mn`
  reports zero today. The licence exists; the line is not written. Until it
  is, "the negative is provable" is a warning, and every `!E` the README
  shows is advisory at the executable.
- `verify`: an interval fragment and a ledger with honest debt, no SMT. The
  field is well ahead here and the design already says so.
- `lower`/`backends`: the twin emission, the repr gradient, the fold leaves
  and the structural equality are the form half done as handlers, which is
  the vision's own answer to "the compiler chooses the form" (§3).
- `lib/persist`, `lib/threading`, `lib/search`: persist is one memory copy,
  threads spawn over the shared image, search is the Choice effect with
  backtracking handlers. The TIME and SPACE substrates exist as library, and
  nothing composes them into the fused search yet.
- `ide/`: the boot in the browser is a real achievement and the page's five
  surfaces are the right ones; the one thing to hold it to is the design
  system's own rule that the projection never takes the keyboard away.
- `tests/`: 480 fixtures prove the compiler. The medium's proposals have zero
  fixtures. A proposal fixture is a hole plus the expected verdict (fill,
  ask with a named divergence, or none); the frontier harness could hold

## 2 · Design: negation sound under polymorphism and across worlds

### 2.1 What the artifact does today, read at the definitions

A row is `EfRow(present, absent, tail)` read as `present ∪ (tail ∖ absent)`,
with the tail `EtClosed`, `EtOpen(edges)` (a set of row-cell handles whose
live content joins in) or `EtAll` (everything except the absent set). Two
different things currently live in the `absent` field:

- **A mask.** `~> h` subtracts `handled(h)`: `diff_row` mints a fresh cell
  bound to `([], mask, EtOpen(edges))` so the subtraction rides every union
  as one opaque edge. This is a *filter on a reading* and it is correct.
- **A prohibition.** A declared `!E` becomes the gate row `([], [E], EtAll)`
  and is checked by `row_subsumes(body, gate)` at declaration exit. When the
  body's tail is open, the declared absences are also written into the
  cell's absent field "so `!E` constrains every instantiation". This is a
  *constraint on a variable*, and it is stored in the same field as the mask.

The gate fires once, at declaration exit, against whatever the body row is at
that moment. When the body row chases to a free cell (the effect-polymorphic
HOF: `fn run(f) with !E = f()` has `row(run) = row(f)`, a parameter's own
free cell), the gate's `NRowFree` arm does nothing, and nothing is written
that a later instantiation could refuse. Measured in RESIDUE on 2026-08-18:
`run(() => op())` compiles with zero diagnostics, and so do the `Pure` and
the unrelated `!WASI` forms. The residue's own conclusion is right: "the
constraint belongs at the DECLARATION, where inference owns the write", and
rejecting free tails at the *read* is the twice-measured dead end (646 false
mismatches on the self-compile). Two more facts shape the design. The ETALL
law: a stored `EtAll` in an inferred row fabricates "everything except", so
`EtAll` belongs in a gate, never in a row value — and the artifact today
writes it into cells at exactly two places, the `Open ~ All` and `All ~ Open`
unify arms, which are its existing idiom for a negative stance on a variable
and become gate installs in §2.2. And the population of the fix: four
declared-row HOFs on the wheel's entry link, counted by the medium's own
`census declared-row-hof` (the eleven RESIDUE recorded on 2026-08-18 were
counted per module and are stale for the entry).

### 2.2 The design: the gate is carried by the cell — second form, after the pass

The first form of this section was refuted by its adversarial pass (§7.3)
with four counterexamples, two of them unsound today with no negation
declared anywhere. What follows is the form that survives them, with the
pass's amendments folded in place. Where the artifact is cited, the line was
re-read this session; the probes are the pass's, re-run inline.

**The representation.** A gate is a constraint on a *cell*, and it must
outlive the cell's binding, because a gated cell that is bound (rule 2) and
later taught (the join arm) or re-finalized (the three `graph_finalize_row`
callers) must still be checked. So the gate is not a payload on `NRowFree`;
it is a **per-handle column beside the node kinds** (the §11 columns law:
a fact about a handle lives in the spine, read live by every writer):

```
gate_col : handle -> Option(EffRow)      // absent for every ungated cell
```

The gate is a declared row in the shape `row_subsumes` already takes as its
second argument: a closed positive row (`with A + B`), a negation row
(`([], [E, F], EtAll)`), or their meet. `EtAll` appears in gates and nowhere
else. Today the `Open ~ All` and `All ~ Open` unify arms (`effects.mn`
`unify_row_canonical`) write `EfRow([], absent, EtAll)` *into* cells — that
is the artifact's existing idiom for a negative capability stance, and it is
what makes an authored `f: () -> Int with !E` refuse today. Under this design
those two arms become gate installs on every edge they reach, and the
invariant "no row *value* is ever `EtAll`" holds by construction. The absent
field keeps exactly one job: the mask.

**Rule 0 — the prerequisite the first form did not know it had: the
nested-frame prune keeps the enclosing signature's cells. LANDED 2026-09-25.**
`row_keep_completion` drops, at every frame exit, a free edge minted during
the judgment unless it is a pre-registration cell or in the frame's keep-set,
and the keep-set was the frame's OWN signature: the tee body and both fanout
thunks exited with `[]`, the lambda with its own parameters only. A
parameter's row cell escapes through the *enclosing* declaration's
signature — the prune's own "third keep category" — and nested frames never
saw it, so `fn run(f) = (f()) ~> h` published `Pure` and a program performing
an effect nobody handles compiled clean and trapped (§7.1). Two forms were
built on scratch copies before one landed, and the first was refuted by the
wheel: no prune at nested exits keeps judgment scratch behind the tee's mask
cell, where the declaration's prune (a bound edge is kept whole) and the
quantifier both miss it, and one caller's effects then leak into a library
function's published row for every later caller — 163 `E_EffectMismatch`
across 18 modules, `GraphRead` inside `lib/lists.mn`. The landed form: each
frame records its signature cells (`inf_frame_params`) and every exit prunes
with the union over the frame stack (`inf_exit_fn` lost its argument), so
the parameter survives and scratch drops. One line later the tee had a
second drop: `read_bound_row`'s `_ => mk_ef_pure()` catch-all read a frame
row whose whole content is one free edge as `Pure` (the single-edge alias
chase lands on the free cell); it answers the edge now. Measured after the
landing: all five prune shapes refuse at the root instead of trapping, one
under-declared wheel signature exposed (`verify_each_enriched` lacked
`Cast`), one dead abort stub exposed and deleted (`Hβ.voice.dead-abort-stub`),
the wheel at zero diagnostics, crown 62/62, the march TRANSITION m3 == m4.
`Hβ.infer.nested-frame-prune-drops-parameter-row` carries the record.

**Rule 1 — the declaration installs the gate before the body is judged.**
The declared row is parse truth (`build_declared_row` needs only the resolved
instance names), so the gate is installed at *pre-registration* on the
declaration's own row cell and on every row variable of its signature — not
at exit. Two things follow. Every charge into the frame row during the body's
judgment is a write into a gated cell and is checked by rule 2 at the site
that charged it, so a violation refuses where it happens, carrying the
declaration's span. And a signature'd or Mycroft self-reference, which
instantiates the pre-registered scheme fresh per use, copies a *gated*
variable by rule 4 — the pass's A3 (`fn run(f: () -> Int, n: Int) -> Int
with !E = if n > 0 { run(() => op(), n - 1) } else { f() }`, accepted today,
`op()` runs) refuses at the recursive call. The exit check survives in one
role: `T_OverDeclared` for a closed positive row the body never reaches.

For a body whose row is already determined at exit nothing changes. For a
body whose resolved row has free terminals, the gate is installed on **every
free terminal edge of the resolved row** (`resolve_row`, never `graph_chase`
— `chase_node` follows a single-edge tail as an alias but stops at a
two-edge cell, and `merge_chased_row` folds a mask's absents forward into a
synthetic `NRowBound`, so the first form's "if the chase ends at `NRowFree`"
never fired for `fn both(f, g) with !E = f() + g()` and never fired through
a mask). Pushing the gate past a mask cell `([], mask, EtOpen(inner))`
removes the mask's names from the gate's absents and, for a closed positive
gate, adds them to its admitted presents: absorption satisfies negation
structurally, which is what `sound-masking-negation` pins, and it is what
makes `fn run(f) with !E + !F = (f()) ~> h` gate `f`'s cell with `!F` alone
rather than refusing the callback `h` absorbs.

The write that stands in the `NRowBound` arm today — joining the declared
absences into the cell's absent field "so `!E` constrains every
instantiation" — is measured **inert** at the caller (`adv-la-two-first-neg`
refuses at `main` through `main`'s own gate; `adv-lb-two-first-root` refuses
at the root gate; neither refusal comes from that write) and it is what makes
the two-parameter shape teach a false `T_OverDeclared` on a body the gate
never judged. It is deleted.

**Rule 2 — every write into a gated cell checks, and pushes.** The pass
enumerated the writers, and there are more than the first form's three:

| writer | what it must do |
|---|---|
| `unify_row_canonical`'s five arms (`Closed~Open`, `Open~Closed`, `Open~Open`, `Open~All`, `All~Open`) | check the value against the target's gate; push the gate onto the value's open edges |
| the one-way argument edge, `infer.mn:4064` (`bind_edges_to(vs, arow)`) — the bind that fires for `run(() => op())` | check against the `chase_deep`-folded argument row; this site is the headline refusal |
| `graph_bind_row`'s JOIN arm, and its occurs-guard drop | a taught cell is re-checked; a silently dropped bind is a checked drop, never a skipped one |
| the three `graph_finalize_row` REPLACE callers (`inf_exit_fn`, the group-completion re-finalize, `enforce_row_gate`'s instance pin) | a cell whose gate was pushed by a caller (the `run(a)` shape) is finalized by REPLACE and must be checked then |
| `diff_row`'s mask mint | fresh cell; the push-through rule above |
| `mint_row` (three sites) and `graph_fresh_row` (twenty-one) | a cell born as a copy of a gated cell is born gated (rule 4) |

A failure is `E_EffectMismatch` at the writing site, carrying the gate's
declaring span, and — the §5 arming — it refuses the executable. The check
is `row_subsumes(value, gate)` on the value *as stored after folding*, so
the read stays a read.

**Rule 3 — var meets var.** In `Open ~ Open`, the edges private to one side
bind to a residual whose tail is the other side's edge set (the callee's row
is the `a` side of `unify_row`, so the callee's private edge aliases the
argument's cell and the argument is the root — the pass killed the
"orientation strands the gate" hypothesis on that read); the binding side's
gates push onto that set. Gates meet as rows meet: presents intersect where
both are closed, absents union, a closed gate meeting a negation gate keeps
the closed presents and the union of absents.

**Rule 4 — the gate is a property of the cell, read live at instantiation.**
`build_inst_mapping` copies the gate when it mints the fresh cell; `mint_row`
carries it; the scheme never freezes it (this is the qualified-type reading,
`∀r. (E ∉ r) ⇒ …`, with the constraint living on the variable rather than in
a context). Mycroft's alpha-stability belt (`ty_alpha_eq`) compares gates
too, or an assumed scheme can lose one between rounds.

**What the reads keep.** `row_subsumes` stays a read. `merge_chased_row`,
`chase_node`, the census, `is_pure` and the gradient see gates only through
the writers that already refused; no projection becomes a writer.

**What it dissolves.** The vacuous class (`fn run(f) with !E = f()` ·
`run(() => op())` refuses at the argument edge with both spans); the
two-parameter and the masked shapes (every free terminal is gated, through
masks); the pre-gate copy (A3); the silent drop at nested frames (rule 0,
which is a live "nothing executes unproven" failure independent of any
negation); the false `T_OverDeclared` on unjudged bodies; the two homes of
`absent`.

**The invariant, restated so the counterexamples cannot recur.** *(a) No row
charge is dropped between a parameter's cell and the frame that publishes
it — the lambda, the tee body and the fanout thunk pass their rows through.
(b) Every cell whose content can flow into a gated cell's reading is gated at
least as strongly, modulo the masks on the path. (c) No gated cell is ever
written a row that fails its gate, at any of the writers above. (d) Every
copy of a gated cell is gated, including the copies a pre-registered or
assumed scheme hands to a self-reference.* Rule 0 is (a); rules 1–3 are (b)
and (c); rule 4 is (d).

**A surface consequence the theorem forces.** A positive declared row on a
HOF becomes a closed cap on its callbacks. The wheel's entry link has four
declared-row HOF sites today (`mentl query src/main.mn "census
declared-row-hof"`: cli:79, env:107, types:3915, search:103 — the "eleven"
RESIDUE counted per module on 2026-08-18 is stale for the entry), and one of
them, `try_with_abort_catch(try_fn, catch_fn) with Abort` in `lib/search.mn`,
will refuse under the theorem: its thunks perform `GraphWrite` and resume
rows, and its own comment says the row was meant "to allow the outer scope's
effects to flow", which a positive row cannot say. That refusal is correct,
and the fix is SYNTAX's own law (§«A signature is not an inventory»): the
positive row on a HOF was never a decision, so it is deleted and inferred,
which makes `Hβ.syntax.positive-row-is-authored-by-hand` a dependency of the
crown arc rather than a style item. The census also undercounts the
population: a parameter *passed* to another HOF (`map(g, xs)`) puts its row
into the body through `g`'s cell and is not a call of the parameter.

### 2.3 The theorem, and the proof it owes

**Statement.** Let `f` declare row `D`. If the program is accepted, then in
every execution, every operation performed during the evaluation of `f`'s
body that is not absorbed by a handler installed within that evaluation has a
name that `D` admits: present in `D`'s positive set when `D` is closed, and
not forbidden by `D`'s absent set under the instance law (`eff_forbids`).
"Absorbed by a handler installed within that evaluation" is coherent for
name-based dynamic innermost dispatch: an `E` performed dynamically inside the
extent meets an `E`-handler at least as inner as the static mask's, so the
static mask never claims an absorption the dynamics denies.

**The base calculus, corrected and verified.** The first form named Biernacki, Piróg, Polesiuk and Sieczkowski (POPL 2019), a scoped-row calculus whose preservation proof does not transfer to set-rows with subtraction. The paper bodies were read on 2026-09-25 (Bauer–Pretnar
LMCS 2014, arXiv 1306.6316; Hillerström–Lindley TyDe 2016): both use effect
signatures as SETS, handler types that subtract the handled operations
(`Hand`: every op in Δ is handled or in Δ′; `A E⇒E′ C`), innermost dispatch
with forwarding, and progress + preservation (Twelf; a substitution-lemma
sketch). Both are MONOMORPHIC — Bauer–Pretnar with subtyping and instances
(`ι#op`, a direct ancestor of `Sample(44100)`), Hillerström–Lindley with row
polymorphism only sketched ("a Rémy-style account… future work"). So the
polymorphic layer with absence — Rémy presence types, Gaster–Jones `lacks` —
combined with handler subtraction has no published proof; lemma (i) is new.
Biernacki et al. POPL 2019 could not be fetched (three hosts returned 403);
its characterization stays marked as from the abstract plus prior knowledge. Flix's λ∁ (ICFP 2023) proves "no excluded effect is ever performed" with no handler construct, so it is the instance of the theorem without lemma (iii), not a proof of it.

**Lemmas.** (i) *gate preservation*: every writer in rule 2 refuses a
violating write and pushes as stated; (ii) *generalization*: a quantified row
variable carries its gate and every instance is minted with it; (iii)
*install soundness*: the mask minted at `~>` corresponds to the handler's
absorbed set under innermost dispatch, and the handler's arms are judged
inside the install's scope (the 2026-08-18 state-init fix is this lemma's
corner); (iv) *resume charges the residual* (§2.4, the static rule); (v)
*exit-bind completeness*: the prune drops only edges that no scheme and no
enclosing signature reaches — false today, rule 0 makes it true; (vi)
*pre-gate instantiation*: no reference ever binds an ungated copy of a
variable the declaration gates — rule 1's pre-registration install; (vii)
*gate persistence*: the gate survives `NRowFree → NRowBound`, the JOIN and
every REPLACE — the column; (viii) *the argument edge is a bind*: the
one-way edge at `infer.mn:4064` folds the argument's row to a value first and
is checked on that value; (ix) *latent versus performed*: abstraction is
pure and the mask subtracts only performed effects, which is what keeps
`leak-escape-negation` sound and `sound-escape-birth-evidence` accepted;
(x) *rule 1 reads through masks*: the install is over `resolve_row`'s free
terminals with the push-through, never over a chased synthetic node.
Progress is unchanged. (i), (v), (vi), (vii) and (x) are the new work.

**Lemma (i) through a mask, done by hand (2026-09-25).**

Setting. A row `(P, A, T)` reads as `P ∪ (⟦T⟧ ∖ A)`; `⟦EtClosed⟧ = ∅`,
`⟦EtAll⟧ = U`, `⟦EtOpen(vs)⟧ = ⋃ ⟦cell(v)⟧`. A gate on a cell is an upper
bound `G ⊆ U` (`⟦(∅, Ag, EtAll)⟧ = U ∖ Ag` for a negation; `⟦(Pg, ∅,
EtClosed)⟧ = Pg` for a closed positive row); "c satisfies G" is
`row_subsumes(G, c)`, i.e. `⟦c⟧ ⊆ G`. Gates meet by intersection.

Invariant I. For every gated cell `c` and every solution `σ` consistent with
the graph's bindings, `σ(c) ⊆ G_c`.

Bind through a mask. Let a gated free cell `c` (gate `G`) be bound to `R =
(P, A, EtOpen(vs))`. Requirement: `∀σ. P ∪ (⋃ σ(v) ∖ A) ⊆ G`. This splits into
a ground check and a push:
- ground: `P ⊆ G`, checked now on the FOLDED value (bound edges already
  folded into `P` by the flat-cell law, so a bound edge carrying a forbidden
  name is caught here — which is why the argument-edge bind must check the
  `chase_deep`-folded row);
- push: for each free `v ∈ vs`, `σ(v) ∖ A ⊆ G ⟺ σ(v) ⊆ G ∪ A`, so
  `G_v := G_v ∩ (G ∪ A)`. For a negation gate `U ∖ Ag` this is `U ∖ (Ag ∖ A)`:
  the mask's names leave the absents. For a closed positive gate `Pg` it is
  `Pg ∪ A`: the mask's names join the admitted presents. That is exactly the
  push-through rule in LENS §2.2, derived rather than asserted.
- a bound `v` (e.g. `diff_row`'s mask cell `(∅, A', EtOpen(inner))`) recurses:
  its inner edges receive `G ∪ A ∪ A'` — masks on a path accumulate by union.

Termination: names are finite; a gate only shrinks by intersection; a
negation gate's absents only grow within the program's finite name set.

Two paths to one cell: `fn run(f) with !E = f() + ((f()) ~> h)` pushes
`U ∖ {E}` on the direct path and `U` on the masked path; the meet is
`U ∖ {E}` — the unmasked call correctly forbids `E`.

Propagation to a caller's cell: in `Open ~ Open` the callee's private edge
aliases the argument's cell, so the callee's gate lands on the caller's cell
(a lambda's row var, or a HOF caller's own parameter). This is `∀r. (E ∉ r)
⇒ …` propagating outward (Gaster–Jones `lacks`), and it is what makes the
crown transitive: `fn outer(k) = run(k)` carries `run`'s gate on `k`.
Correct by the theorem statement — the promise is per evaluation of the
declaring body; an outer handler does not count.

No false-mismatch class: the twice-measured dead end (646 false mismatches)
came from refusing a FREE tail at a read; a gate refuses only a write that
contradicts it. Consequently `T_OverDeclared` must not narrate on a body
whose resolved row has free terminals — the narration requires a ground row.

`Pure` needs no special case: a closed empty gate `∅` refuses `[E]`, admits
`Pure`, pushes `∅` onto any HOF row it meets.

Instances: `!Sample(44100)` as a gate uses `eff_forbids`; a provably distinct
instance is admitted, a non-provably-distinct one refused — the
conservative side, consistent with today's arm.

What remains mechanical: (a) the writer set is complete (LENS §2.7's table);
(b) the quantifier's free-var walk reaches cells behind bound mask cells
exactly as `flatten_row_edges` does (the three-walks-must-agree law) — and
variant A above is the measured cost of a walk that does NOT (a scratch cell
behind a mask, neither dropped nor quantified); (c) `T_OverDeclared` gated on
groundness. No fifth counterexample was found by hand in twelve searches
(two-path meet, escaping closures, mutual recursion under pre-registration
gates, var-meets-var, `Pure`, masks over shared cells, the decl-exit prune,
the root gate, handler arms, fanout thunks, `TCont`, instances).

### 2.4 Across worlds: what the artifact holds, the rule, and what it waits on

**What the artifact holds** (re-read this session). `TCont(R, S, disc,
world)`'s world is minted per arm inside `register_handler`'s own frame as
`inf_current_world()`, which is *the current frame's live row cell* — the
handler's `r_handle`, not any performer's remainder. `ResumeExpr` reads only
`R` and `S` from the continuation and nothing from any boundary; the
per-call-site boundaries (`stage_continuation_boundary`) capture the
performer's whole frame cell and are read by no resume. There is no
first-class `k` (`E_ResumeOutsideArm`), no two-argument `resume(k, v)`, and
`mentl resume <image>` restores a whole image, not a continuation under a
different handler set. The first form's fixture `fn later() with !E =
resume(k, 1)` is not writable, and its rule "world = the residual of the
remainder, charged at resume" would, if plumbed, turn `sound-masking-negation`
red: for `fn work() = op() + op()` under `fn f() with !E = (work()) ~> h`,
the remainder's row is `[E]`, the arm's `resume(1)` would charge `[E]` into
`row(h)`, the tee adds `row(h)` back, and `f`'s `!E` refuses a program the
crown accepts today, correctly.

**The rule.** Resuming inside an arm of `h` continues the remainder *under
`h`*, so what the arm's row must carry is the remainder's requirement with
`h`'s own absorption removed:

```
row(resume(v)) inside an arm of h, for a perform site p
  = ( ⋃ rows of every frame from p up to the nearest lexically enclosing
      absorbing tee ) − handled(h)
```

Under dynamic innermost dispatch the true delimiter may be an inner handler,
which makes the real remainder *shorter* than the static union — so the
static rule over-approximates the row and never under-approximates it, which
is the sound direction. Its gates are `sound-masking-negation` and the pass's
`adv-arm-resume-world` (an arm doing `resume(fop())`, row `F`), both of
which must stay green.

**What it waits on.** The world's load-bearing use is a *reified* or
*persisted* continuation resumed somewhere else, and that needs a
first-class continuation value — band B's own item. When it exists, the
image stores the suspended frames' row *value* beside the record, and
`mentl resume` refuses at load, with a located `E_ResumeWorldMismatch` naming
the captured world and the resuming site's ambient world, when the ambient
world does not subsume it. That is the typed refusal at the deserialization
boundary, and it is still the target; the first form wrote it as though the
carrier existed. It does not, and PLAN §7's "`TCont` effect-WORLD is INERT on
OneShot" is the honest line until it does.

### 2.5 Install identity: not one arm, but band A's capability-passing design

The first form said a handler value could be an instance argument and that
`eff_args_provably_distinct` needed one arm. Re-read at the definitions,
three things stand in the way, and together they are the modal world-index,
not an arm:

- **Masks are by name.** `derive_handler_enames` builds `ENamed`; `diff_row`
  subtracts through `name_set_diff` → `eff_name_handle` equality. So `~> h2`
  subtracts `E(h1)` too. An instance-precise mask means `diff_row` subtracts
  `E(h)` only for the matching install.
- **Handler values are never provably distinct.** `eff_args_provably_distinct`
  decides scalars and the registration constants (`EAEffect`/`EACon`) by
  intern handle; a let-bound or parameter-bound handler value is `EANode`,
  never distinct, so `!E(h)` would forbid every `E`. Two installs of *one*
  declaration — the real accidental-capture shape, a recursive function
  installing the same handler at each level — are the same `EACon`.
  Distinctness has to be defined by INSTALL, not by declaration.
- **The perform site would need the install identity at runtime.** Lower
  dispatches through the live world chain, not the row; "lexical capture
  when the program names the install" is only true if the perform carries
  the install's handle in its evidence and the chain resolves to it or
  refuses.

So the design is stated as the three-part thing it is: the install edge's
handle is the identity; a row may carry `E(install)`; `diff_row` and
`eff_forbids` are instance-precise over installs; the perform site's evidence
carries the install handle. Dynamic innermost dispatch stays the default and
lexical binding is what a program writes when it names the install — that
part of the first form stands — but it is `Hβ.effects.modal-world-index` with
its dependencies named, and it is sequenced after the gate, not with it.

## 3 · Design: is the e-graph the most powerful thing for the form half?

### 3.1 What is built

`egraph.mn` is 318 lines. Equivalence is one more graph edge (`graph_canon_set`
writes one canon cell per handle, chased by `graph_canon_at`), every rule
reads its operands through the chase so a rewrite composes across nesting
(`(0 + x) * 1` lowers to `x`, measured), and a dropping rewrite fires only
when the dropped operand is a literal, a variable or arithmetic over those
*and* its type row is pure. Six rules: the additive and multiplicative
identities, absorption by zero, subtraction and division identities, and
constant folding. Every rule points a node at a descendant or a fresh leaf,
so each class has one *root*, the rewritten originals stay in the graph as
unreachable members, and "extraction" is the chase. Two things the adversarial
pass measured against the file's own comments: the canon cell is overwritten
at equal cost (`0 + 0` draws `g → minted 0` on pass one and `g → the literal
operand` on pass two, counted as a productive pass), and a `with Pure` *call*
is never dropped, because the shape test refuses every call, so the comment
"a Pure-typed call IS droppable" is false. `saturate_range` is called on the
fan's candidate range, but no enumerator mints a `BinOpExpr`, so on the fan
path it can never draw an edge: the form half of the fork/merge duality is a
design today, not a mechanism. What is built is a directed simplifier with an
effect gate and a good home, not equality saturation: nothing keeps two forms
of one class alive to be chosen between later, which is the entire reason
equality saturation exists.

### 3.2 The honest answer

Three things in the design are right and should be kept exactly.

- **The fork/merge duality.** Meaning is explored by forking (candidates
  conflict, so each verifies in a rolled-back world); form is explored by
  merging (members are equal by construction, so nothing is undone). A tie
  in form is free and a tie in meaning is a question. This is the principle
  that makes the oracle and the optimizer one substrate, and it is not in
  the literature in that form.
- **Effect-gated rewriting, once partiality is in the row.** egg rewrites
  syntactically; the field handles effects by relaxing a control-flow
  skeleton. Reading each operand's row and refusing a dropping rewrite unless
  the row subsumes pure is cleaner, and the adversarial pass found the exact
  place it is currently unsound: the row carries no partiality, so `(1 / 0)
  * 0` and `(1 / z) * 0` with `z = 0` compile to `0` while the control `1 /
  z` traps (measured, three modules). The fold rule's own comment says "the
  trap is the program's meaning, not a constant"; the absorb rule then
  deletes that meaning. The Mentl-shaped fix is not a special case in the
  gate: **partiality is an effect.** Division and modulo perform `Trap`
  unless the divisor's refinement proves it nonzero (`NonZero = Int where
  self != 0`), the refinement being the proof exactly as it is everywhere
  else; a row that carries `Trap` does not subsume pure, so the gate refuses
  the drop, and a divisor the developer has proved makes the operation total
  and droppable. That turns a miscompile into a teachable annotation, which
  is the gradient's own move.
- **Equivalence with a Reason.** Every canon edge is a graph edge and can
  carry why it was drawn. No production optimizer can answer "why was my code
  compiled this way" with a walkable chain. That is human-empowering in the
  vision's own sense and it costs nothing extra.

Two things should not be done next.

- **A whole-program saturating optimizer.** Extraction with shared subterms
  is NP-hard and hard to approximate (OOPSLA 2024); saturation blows up;
  static costs mislead on real hardware, which is why AlphaDev and STOKE
  measure. Building that engine competes for the same hours as the
  proposer, the gradient and the cursor, and its payoff is codegen quality
  that no program yet asks for.
- **Treating the e-graph as the vision's "compiler chooses the form".** The
  form choices the developer can see and steer are already handlers: the
  schedule (`Seq | Thread | Simd | Gpu`) read live at the install, the repr
  gradient pinning widths, the twin emission. Those are the human-empowering
  half, because the gradient can name them and the developer can accept them.
  Saturation is opaque by nature.

### 3.3 The ultimate form, when a program asks for it

- **One union-find for types and terms.** The graph already holds an HM
  union-find over type cells with a Reason on every merge. Term cells are the
  same mechanism one kind over: a class is a cell, a member is an edge, a
  merge is a bind with a Reason, and extraction is a read. There is no e-graph
  data structure because there is no separate type union-find either; the
  `egraph.mn` comment already says this, and the design is to mean it: keep
  members when a rule is non-monotone, keep the directed chase when it is.
- **Cost is a handler, never on the default chain.** Extraction's cost
  function is `~> Cost`: static size for the fan's dedup, a cycle model for
  the emit, and *measured latency* through the runner for the audio path.
  The adversarial pass priced it: the exec seam exists (`mentl_host.exec`
  returns an exit code; each candidate must be a whole module; the runner's
  Cranelift compile is paid per candidate), the guest has no clock import,
  so a timed exec op or a clock is new host surface, and a measured cost must
  land as a graph fact with provenance and an epoch, never be read live. The
  determinism hazard is structural: the fixpoint is byte identity and run
  variance is measured at ±14%, so measured cost may steer emission only
  under an explicitly installed `~> Cost`, and the artifact it produces is
  host-dependent by construction. The pass also found, beside this, that the
  warm image bakes the verb's handler chain: `mentl compile` then `mentl run`
  on the same file prints WAT and exits 134, and `run` then `compile` prints
  nothing. A `~> Cost` installed in one session would restore into the next.
  That is a live defect in the warm path and it is listed in §7.
- **Tractable extraction from the surface: a conjecture, refuted as stated.**
  The draft of this section claimed that programs in the five verbs are
  series-parallel with bounded feedback, so a structure-preserving rule set
  keeps the e-graph at bounded treewidth and the OOPSLA 2024 algorithm
  (optimal extraction for treewidth up to about ten, on the *saturated
  e-graph*) makes optimal extraction polynomial. The adversarial pass
  refuted both halves with constructions that compile and run today. The
  premise fails on variable sharing, not on the verbs: `fn k(a, b, c, d) =
  (a + b + c + d) >< (a * b * c * d) >< …` has a K₄,₄ minor in its dataflow,
  treewidth at least four, and generalizes without bound. The rule half
  fails on the one rule named as safe: reassociation over an n-stage chain
  saturates to the interval e-graph, whose classes `C[1, j]` form a clique
  minor of size n − 1, so treewidth grows with chain length in any
  encoding; this is the associativity blow-up egg's own paper discusses.
  What survives is smaller and still useful: extraction is trivial when the
  rule set is directed and confluent (the chase, which is what is built),
  and bounded when saturation is depth-bounded, with the bound depending on
  the iteration limit and on the number of variables live across a fanout,
  not on a constant. "Extraction gets smaller" is therefore true of the
  *rule set* (rows forbid rewrites, repr pins widths) and false of the
  *graph*, and the sentence in PLAN §11.1 should say so.

**Verdict.** The e-graph is the right principle and the wrong next build. Keep
the fork/merge duality, the equivalence edge with a Reason, and the effect
gate; put partiality in the row before anything else touches the gate; grow
the rule set only when Pulse's audio path asks (fusion of adjacent stages is
the first rule a renderer will want) and keep it directed for as long as
that is enough, since a directed rule set is the one case where extraction
is free; make cost a handler off the default chain; and retire the
treewidth guarantee, which was arithmetic about the wrong graph.

## 4 · Design: the ranker, and what "the medium proposes" can honestly mean

The measurements (audit §4.4) are unambiguous: where proof search and learned
ranking have been compared, learned ranking wins by wide margins, and every
best result is a hybrid in which proof filters and a model ranks. The vision
does not need to lose to this; it needs to say what is uniquely its own.

- **The filter is the medium's and it is total.** Nothing surfaces that did
  not survive inference and proof in a rolled-back world. A ranker, whatever
  it is, chooses among proven things. That sentence is already true of the
  built proposer and it is stronger than any claim about models.
- **Constraints compound; guesses do not. This is a design, not yet a
  mechanism.** Measured: accepting a proposal today is a text patch
  (`apply_suggestion` splices the file and re-checks), the `Reason` type has
  no constructor for an accepted or rejected proposal, and the rejected list
  is rendered and dropped. The design is that every acceptance and every
  answered divergence lands as an edge (a `Proposal` Reason on the filled
  node; the answer as a constraint on the cell), so the next proposal at the
  same and neighbouring positions is narrower: an answered question narrows
  the space for this project for good, while a model's ranking never
  becomes a constraint. Over a project's life the medium's proposer then
  converges toward the project's intent while a model plateaus at its corpus.
  That is ULTIMATE_MEDIUM §8.4's "the gradient learns THIS project", and the
  edge is the whole of its mechanism.
- **The space is small exactly when the constraints are, and that is a
  measurement, not a slogan.** Measured this session on the built fan: a
  hole of type `Positive` (a refinement) yields one survivor and fills; a
  bare `Int` hole in a scope with twenty nullary integer functions yields 23
  survivors and asks a question that can only say "nothing here bounds the
  value"; a function-typed hole in a pipe stage (`5 |> ??` with three
  unary functions in scope) yields *no proposal at all*, because the
  vocabulary enumerator proposes zero-argument calls and never a function
  reference or a stage. So the honest shape of the claim is: at next-move
  scope the medium wins when the position carries a real constraint, asks
  usefully only when the divergence has content, and today cannot see the
  most common shape in a Mentl program, the pipe stage. Lean's `exact?` is
  the evidence that enumeration plus proof beats generation on constrained
  goals; the gradient's job is to make positions constrained (that is what
  "annotations are inputs" means), and the proposer's first growth is the
  stage.
- **Ranking is a handler, and the medium's own ranker is symbolic and
  explainable.** `rank_of` reads proximity today, and nothing else. The
  design adds two signals no model has because they are project-local
  provenance: *Reason overlap* (how much of a candidate's Reason chain
  shares ancestry with the constraints stated near the hole, so "proposed
  because it carries the `Sample` refinement you declared at line 12" is a
  true sentence) and *acceptance history* (what this developer took and
  refused at this shape before). Neither is computable from what the graph
  stores now: a candidate's Reason is an `Inferred` string, `Reason` is a
  value tree of strings and spans rather than handle-keyed edges (PLAN §11's
  own complaint), and there is no proposal edge. Both become computable the
  day the `Proposal` edge exists and Reasons carry handles. Cost from
  `~> Cost` breaks form ties. Every signal renders as a sentence at the
  address surface.
- **A model plugs in behind the gate and is measured on the same fixture.**
  The Synth chain already admits `synth_llm` as a peer handler. The design
  adds the honest consequence: the medium keeps the gradient log (proposal,
  verdict, what was written) as a fixture, and any ranker, symbolic or
  learned, local or remote, is scored on it. A tiny local ranker trained on
  the project's own accepted moves is the vision-consistent form of a
  learned ranker: private, offline, grown from constraints.

**The literature, scoped correctly.** The adversarial pass corrected the
audit's reading in the vision's favour. Learned ranking wins at whole-proof
and premise-selection scope (Magnushammer, LeanDojo's ReProver, Lean
Copilot). At *precise-specification next-move* scope, the closest analogue to
a typed hole with a refinement, the published comparisons go the other way:
Li, Parsert and Polgreen (CAV 2024) report cvc5's enumerative solver at 68.1%
against GPT-3.5 alone at 49%, with the hybrid at 80.1%; Egolf, Zhou and
Tripakis (arXiv 2603.20264, March 2026) find across reactive, SyGuS,
protocol and recursive-function synthesis that "the symbolic tools solve
more benchmarks than Qwen and either outperform or are about on par with
GPT-5", and are faster. Canonical (ITP 2025) has no model arm and is not
evidence either way; no published `exact?`-versus-model next-move comparison
exists, and the draft's sentence citing it is withdrawn.

**The claim, stated so it survives the evidence.** The medium owns the
candidate space and the verdict; proof is the filter; at precise-spec
next-move scope enumeration plus proof is ahead of models in every published
comparison and the hybrid is ahead of both; ranking is a handler whose
default is symbolic and explainable and whose memory will be the graph once
the proposal edge exists; a tie the ranker cannot break is a question, and
the answer compounds. "No model is advantageous at any scope" is still
retired: the scope where the vision wins is named, and Pulse's next-move
benchmark is where the number comes from.

## 6 · What this document could not close

- A mechanized proof of §2.3. The base is now Bauer–Pretnar rather than the
  scoped-row calculus, and the lemmas are ten rather than four; no published
  proof combines `lacks`-style gates with handler subtraction, so lemma (i)
  through the mask, and (v)–(vii) on the artifact's own writers, are done by
  hand and are where a fifth counterexample would live.
- Whether rule 0's pass-through at nested frames re-opens the 4GB
  generalize∘instantiate class the prune was built against. The argument
  that it cannot (nothing generalizes at a nested exit) is a reading; the
  march's peak-RSS ratchet is the measurement.
- The choice between dynamic innermost dispatch and lexical capture as the
  *default*. §2.5 keeps the current default and makes lexical binding an
  instance the program writes. A capability calculus would make the other
  choice; the crown's crucibles pin this one, and Pulse will show whether
  two live handlers for one effect is a shape the founding workload needs.
- The treewidth conjecture of §3.3, and whether the rule set Pulse needs
  (stage fusion) preserves the bound under `<|` sharing.
- Whether the symbolic ranker of §4 beats a model on Pulse's holes. That is
  the benchmark's job, and the design is written so that losing it changes
  a handler, not the medium.

## 7 · The adversarial pass

Two Fable passes were dispatched against §2 and against §3–§4, with
instructions to refute by constructing programs and running them. The first
dispatch was killed by the session's rate limit before either read a file;
both were relaunched when usage returned. Their reports are folded in below
as they arrive. The measurements that did not need a fresh mind were taken
inline first.

The mask and the escaped-call cases are right, which is what lets the design

the bind.

| `fn main() with !E = both(() => 1, () => op())` | **refuses** at `main` — the CALLER's gate works, because `main`'s body row is ground |

Two things in that table are new, and they are worse than the vacuous gate.
The tee-over-a-parameter shape and the lambda-over-a-parameter shape carry
**no negation at all** — nothing was declared, nothing was contradicted, the
row was simply LOST between the parameter's cell and the function's
published row, and the only refusal the crown relies on at the root
(`E_EffectUnhandled`) is blind to what it cannot see. The monomorphic tee
and the bare alias are correct, and the authored parameter row is correct,
which bounds the class to *a parameter's free row cell passing through one
intermediate cell* (the tee's mask cell; the lambda's own frame cell). The
mechanism is the pass's to pin (§7.3); the DESIGN consequence does not wait
for it: §2's invariant, as first written, said the gate is carried by the
cell — and that presumes the cell still carries the parameter's row. Here it
does not, so a gate on it would discharge vacuously by the same route. The
invariant §2.3 must prove is therefore stronger than "every gate is checked
at bind": **no row charge is dropped between a parameter's cell and the
frame's published row** — the lambda and the tee are the two constructors
that today stand between them, and the theorem has to name every such
constructor.

### 7.3 The fresh-mind passes

**On §3 and §4 (the form half and the ranker).** The pass ran twenty probes
through the shim and read the WAT. Verdicts: the classification of the
e-graph as a directed simplifier is supported, with "one member per class"
corrected to "one root per class"; the claim that the fan's survivors are
collapsed by saturation is refuted (no candidate can match a rule; the form
half is dead code on the fan path); the effect gate is shape ∧ row, a
`Pure` call is never dropped, and the gate is unsound on partial operations
(`(1 / 0) * 0` → `0`, a measured miscompile); the treewidth conjecture is
refuted twice (variable sharing across `><` branches, and reassociation on a
chain); measured cost is feasible with a time channel and only off the
default chain; "acceptances land as Reason edges" is a design, not a
mechanism (accepting a proposal is a text patch); the candidate space grows
linearly with same-typed vocabulary (23, 33) and the small cases are
artifacts of the arity-zero enumerator and of the hole's allowed row being
the authored row; ranking is proximity alone, confirmed; Reason overlap and
acceptance history are not computable from what is stored; and the
literature sentence was overstated against the vision at next-move scope
(the CAV 2024 and March 2026 results have symbolic enumeration ahead of
models on precise specifications). Every one of these is folded into §3
and §4 above. Defects the pass found beside the claims, each worth a peer:
the warm image restores the previous verb's handler chain (`compile` then
`run` prints WAT and exits 134); the absorb rule drops a trap; the hole's
allowed row is the authored row; `extract_chase` returns a non-root
silently at its depth cap; the canon column has no projection, so an
equal-cost overwrite is unobservable except by reading the code.

**On §2 (negation).** The pass ran twenty-eight probes through the shim
(`adv-*`), re-ran the crown (62/62 green on the boot as the baseline), and
read every writer of a row cell. Verdict: **gate-on-cell was refuted as
written**, with four counterexamples, and it survives only with the
amendments now folded into §2.2 — the five refutations and the mechanism
behind each: (A1) a body with two callback edges never reaches the
`NRowFree` arm, because `chase_node` follows a single-edge tail as an alias
and stops at two, so `fn both(f, g) with !E = f() + g()` was never gated;
(A2) a mask in the chain makes `merge_chased_row` fold the absents into a
synthetic `NRowBound`, so the gate was either never installed (unsound for
the uncovered negation) or installed without the push-through
(over-refusing the covered one); (A3) a signature'd or Mycroft
self-reference instantiates the pre-registered scheme fresh per use, and
the gate was written only at exit onto the declaration's own cell, so the
copy binds `[E]` ungated — `adv-sigd-self-rec` accepts and runs today; (A4)
nested frames exit with an empty keep-set, so the completion prune drops
the parameter's row before any gate could see it — two programs with no
negation anywhere compile clean and trap; (A5) `TCont`'s world is the
handler's frame cell, not a remainder, `resume` reads no world, no
first-class `k` exists, and the residual-at-resume rule as first written
would have turned `sound-masking-negation` red; (A6) masks are by name and
handler values are never provably distinct, so install-as-instance is the
whole modal world-index, not one arm. The pass also corrected the base
calculus (Bauer–Pretnar, not the scoped-row calculus), named the `lacks`
lineage of the gate, and enumerated the writers (§2.7). Its own killed
hypotheses, recorded so nobody re-chases them: mutual/self recursion lets
the callback bind into a private copy — refuted for unsignatured
declarations (`adv-mutual`, `adv-unsigd-self-rec` refuse; the mono view
shares the cells) and true only for signatured ones; the D2 absent write
launders a violating callback at the caller — refuted, it is inert in both
directions; the `EtOpen ~ EtOpen` orientation strands the gated cell —
refuted, the callee is the `a` side and the argument's cell is the root.
Two more, killed by the artifact while the fix was built (2026-09-25):
that nested frames could simply stop pruning — refuted by the wheel at 163
mismatches, the scratch-behind-a-mask class above; and that the tee shape
would follow from the prune fix — refuted by the probe (`run` still `Pure`),
which named `read_bound_row`'s catch-all. A third, cheaper one: the first
inherited-keep-set patch left two frame literals without the new field, two
`E_TypeMismatch` the unarmed class let compile, and the result trapped on
every program — `E_TypeMismatch` in the wheel's own source must refuse.
Two of mine, killed inline before the report arrived: that let-generalization
over-quantified the lambda's row — killed by `fn run(f) = (() => f())()`
pruning without any `let`; and that the flatten or the least-solution cut
closed the variable — killed by reading `flatten_row_edges` (free edges
survive) and by the control `adv-tee-direct-kept`, where a named callee's
pre-registration cell survives the same exit. The kill that mattered came
from the control: the difference between a surviving edge and a dropped one
was the ceiling, which is what named the prune.
